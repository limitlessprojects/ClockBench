# The ladder (measured against the thesis)

## 90 days (by ~2027-01-22)
- ClockBench v0.1 spec reviewed in public; parity lane runs end-to-end on
  >= 3 clocks x >= 2 implementations x 3 manifest datasets
- Signed scoreboard live; leakage ledger complete for the roster
- >= 10 merged PRs across pyaging / biolearn / methylclock (parity fixes,
  docs, dataset loaders — we help upstream first)
- Preprint v1 drafted and posted to bioRxiv
- First validation-report conversation opened with >= 2 consumer-test companies
- 5+ recurring customers or 2 signed reports (business reality check)

## 1 year
- The benchmark is cited by >= 1 external lab or trial protocol
- Preprint published; >= 1 named collaboration with a package or lab
- Business covers its own costs; 20% sweep alive every month
- First small check deployed into a longevity company
- CI-run scoreboard refreshed on schedule with historical artifacts

## 5 years
- ClockBench is infrastructure the field routes through (the default
  citation when a clock reports performance)
- A small collective (maintainers, reviewers, fellows) runs the referee work
- Validation services + sponsorship fund the operation
- Writing checks into longevity companies from the deployment engine —
  not asking for them

## 10 years
- Running the tooling/standards company the field depends on, funding the
  field, or both. In the room.
- The honest ceiling: not immortality — wealth, credibility, access, and a
  real shot at contributing to (and benefiting from) the healthspan work
  that actually is happening. Aim at that, ruthlessly.
