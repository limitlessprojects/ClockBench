# ClockBench — business books & entity package (v0.1)

## The till rule (from day one, even at $0)

1. **Separation**: revenue lives in a dedicated account (or a clearly
   separated ledger until the account exists). Personal and till never mix.
2. **P&L skeleton** (one sheet, monthly close on the 1st):
   - Revenue: validation reports | benchmark sponsorship | grants | data products
   - Direct costs: compute (CI eval runs), datasets/deposition fees, contractor review
   - Opex: domains, entity fees, accounting
   - **Deployment sweep: 20% of net profit -> the deployment engine** (a
     separate, tracked reserve; invests into longevity companies per
     ROADMAP.md, never into operating expenses)
3. Every number in the P&L maps to a bank line; no memory-based accounting.

## Revenue lanes (nearest-first)

- **Lane 1 (90-day target): paid validation reports** for consumer epigenetic
  testing companies (the market showing $2B/yr, DTC = 40% share, and a public
  trust problem: "same sample, different ages"). Deliverable: neutral parity +
  calibration report against ClockBench v1.0, private, fix-window, then public
  seal if fixes land. Price band: $5k-$25k per report at v1.0 credibility.
- **Lane 2: trial-support analytical validation packages** for sponsors using
  clocks as exploratory endpoints (no FDA-qualified epigenetic biomarker
  exists; qualification support is the wedge).
- **Lane 3: sponsorship** of the public scoreboard (like MLPerf membership).
- **Lane 4 (later): grants + data products.** The open benchmark is the
  credibility engine, never the paid product itself.

## Entity decision package (human-executed; ~1 day)

- **Now**: nothing to file. No revenue = no entity; the repo + ORCID identity
  carry the work. (Professional judgment: entities cost money and attention;
  file when Lane 1 signs.)
- **At first paid engagement**: Wyoming LLC (cheap, fast, privacy) — name
  candidates: `ClockBench Labs LLC` / `Limitless Standards LLC`. Operating
  agreement: IP (the benchmark) stays open; services revenue is the LLC's.
- **At institutional capital or equity deployment**: Delaware C-Corp conversion.
- **Domains** (checked 2026-10-24): `clockbench.org` AVAILABLE,
  `clockbench.io` AVAILABLE, `clockbench.dev` AVAILABLE (`.com` taken —
  don't chase it; benchmarks live on .org). Register .org + .io now (~$60/yr).
- **ORCID** (human, 10 min): register at orcid.org → `0000-000X` iD → link
  the first preprint when posted.
- **Google Scholar** (human, after preprint): Scholar profile auto-claims via
  the preprint's indexed record; add the repo link in the homepage field.
- **GitHub identity**: done — `limitlessprojects/ClockBench`.

## The one-line P&L goal

By month 6: till covers costs from Lane 1; 20% sweep has its first deposit.
By month 12: two validation clients + one sponsorship; engine funds its first
small check into a longevity company (ROADMAP.md year-1 milestone).
